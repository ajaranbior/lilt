How it works

English melody hangs on stressed syllables. The biggest one in a phrase is the nucleus: that's where the tune (fall, rise, …) starts, and everything after it is the tail, which just carries the tune through to the end.

The hum strips the words away so you hear only the melody and rhythm: thick and loud means stressed, thin means unstressed. The line shows the same pitch you hear.
